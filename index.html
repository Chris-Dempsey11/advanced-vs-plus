Side-by-Side Legal Comparison
Area Advanced (Self-Serve ToS) Plus (Negotiated MSA)
Indemnification One-way. Customer indemnifies Shopify only. Shopify carries no indemnification obligations to the merchant. Mutual. Shopify indemnifies the merchant against IP infringement claims and DPA breaches. Risk shared equally.
Liability Cap Not specified. No contractual ceiling on Shopify's liability — effectively uncapped under standard boilerplate. Capped at 12 months of Platform Fees paid. Merchant has a defined, predictable ceiling on recoverable damages.
Confidentiality None. No confidentiality provisions. Business data shared with Shopify carries no contractual protection. Full mutual obligations. Both parties are bound by confidentiality, with injunctive relief available as a remedy for breach.
IP License to Shopify Broad, open-ended grant. Merchant grants Shopify a wide IP license with no defined end date or scope restriction. Scoped and time-limited. License is restricted to the duration of the Term and solely to provide the Services.
Bottom line: Advanced is a click-through, self-serve agreement — protections are one-sided in Shopify's favour. Plus is a negotiated commercial contract with mutual indemnification, a defined liability cap, confidentiality obligations, and joint control over amendments. For any merchant with legal, procurement, or compliance involvement in the purchase decision, the Plus MSA is a materially different document from what they'd be signing on Advanced.

Shopify Audiences is a Plus-exclusive app that uses Shopify's unique commerce data — aggregated across millions of buyers — to generate high-converting ad audiences for your paid campaigns. It helps you find buyers who are most likely to purchase your products, reducing your cost per acquisition and improving ROAS across every major ad platform.

High-Intent Buyer Targeting Plus

Prospecting & Retargeting Plus
Audiences covers both acquisition and retention use cases. Prospecting lists find new buyers who look like your best customers. Retargeting lists re-engage visitors and past purchasers across platforms — all powered by Shopify's commerce graph rather than pixel-based tracking.
Business Impact: Your retargeting audiences are less effective than they were before iOS 14 restrictions. Shopify Audiences restores that targeting power with first-party purchase data — audiences built from real buying behaviour, not browser history.
🔒

Business Impact: As privacy regulations tighten globally, your ad targeting strategy needs to be built on compliant foundations. Shopify Audiences is privacy-safe by design — giving you durable targeting that won't create legal or reputational risk as regulations evolve.

⚡

Business Impact: Your competitors using Meta and Google's native audiences are working with increasingly degraded signals. Shopify Audiences is powered by purchase-intent data from millions of real transactions — a more durable, more specific targeting foundation that platform-native audiences simply can't match.

What are Expansion Stores?
Expansion Stores are additional Shopify storefronts included with every Plus plan — up to 9, all under a single contract and billing relationship. Each expansion store is a fully independent Shopify environment with its own products, themes, domains, apps, staff, and settings, while sharing the same Plus subscription. Advanced merchants get one store, full stop.

Use Cases
🌍
International Markets Plus
Run a fully localised storefront per region — separate domain, currency, language, product catalogue, pricing, and tax rules for each market. Unlike Shopify Markets (which adapts one store), expansion stores give you complete independence: different themes, different product ranges, different fulfilment logic per region.
Business Impact: Entering markets with distinct compliance requirements — like the EU, Australia, or the UK post-Brexit — often requires true store separation, not just a localisation overlay. Expansion stores give you that separation within your Plus contract at no additional platform cost.
🏭
Dedicated B2B Portal Plus
Run a separate B2B storefront entirely isolated from your DTC store — different branding, pricing structure, checkout flow, and customer accounts. B2B buyers get a tailored experience without any risk of DTC pricing or content bleeding through. No hacks, no conditional display logic, no shared cart contamination.
Business Impact: Running B2B and DTC channels under one Shopify contract keeps your operations clean and costs predictable. With Plus, you get two fully independent commerce environments — one bill, one support relationship, complete channel separation.
🏷️
Multi-Brand Architecture Plus
Businesses managing multiple brands can give each brand its own storefront — independent identity, separate product catalogue, dedicated team access, and distinct analytics. No bleed between brands, no shared customer pools, no compromised brand integrity from running everything on one store.
Business Impact: If you're acquiring brands or launching sub-brands, Advanced means paying for a separate Shopify subscription for each. Plus includes up to 9 expansion stores — giving you the infrastructure to grow your brand portfolio without multiplying your platform costs.
🧪
Development & Staging Environments Plus
Use expansion stores as dedicated development and staging environments — mirror your production store's theme, app configuration, and checkout logic in a sandboxed environment before pushing changes live. Test new theme builds, Checkout Extensions, third-party integrations, and app configurations with zero risk to production traffic or live orders.
Business Impact: On Advanced, testing major changes means either risking your live store or paying for a second Shopify subscription. Plus gives you dedicated staging environments included in your plan — so you can test confidently without putting your live business at risk.
🔗
Integration & ERP Testing Plus
For merchants running complex integrations — ERP syncs, OMS, PIM, 3PL, or custom middleware — a dedicated integration test store lets your technical team validate data flows, webhook behaviour, order routing logic, and API edge cases without touching live data. Run full end-to-end integration tests in a production-equivalent environment.
Business Impact: If you're running a complex ERP integration — NetSuite, SAP, Dynamics — a failed sync in production can mean missed orders, incorrect inventory, or broken fulfilment. A dedicated staging store lets you validate every integration change before it touches live data.
🚀
New Market Launch Staging Plus
Before launching into a new market, build and validate the full storefront experience in an expansion store — localised content, payment methods, shipping zones, tax configuration, and checkout flow — without any customer-facing exposure. Hand over a fully configured store to the go-live team rather than building in production.
Business Impact: Rushing a market launch without a validated environment is one of the most common causes of go-live issues. Building your new market storefront in an expansion store lets your team deliver a fully tested, production-ready experience — reducing time to revenue and launch risk simultaneously.
🎨
Theme Development & A/B Testing Plus
Use an expansion store as a dedicated theme development environment — build and iterate on a new storefront design with real products and real checkout flows, without risking the live site. Validate conversion impact before cutting over. Also supports headless commerce prototyping using Shopify's Storefront API.
Business Impact: Theme migrations are high-stakes — any mistake goes live immediately on Advanced. With an expansion store as your development environment, your team can build and test the new theme to production quality before a single customer sees it.
📦
Wholesale & Distributor Portals Plus
Create dedicated portals for specific wholesale tiers, distributors, or channel partners — each with its own product catalogue, pricing, and ordering experience. Different distributors can have completely separate stores with no visibility into each other's pricing or product access.
Business Impact: If you sell across multiple distribution channels, protecting pricing integrity is critical. Expansion stores give you complete separation — your direct buyers see their pricing, your wholesale partners see theirs, with no risk of cross-channel exposure.
